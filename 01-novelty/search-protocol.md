## Screening

Inclusion requires at least one claim-matched mechanism: AI/inference receipt, evidence typing, authority or freshness enforcement, smart-contract settlement, non-compensating decision logic, provenance binding, adversarial replay/substitution tests, or a close implementation lifecycle.

Exclusion reasons are: lexical collision, generic blockchain survey, unrelated financial credit scoring, unrelated lattice cryptography, metadata-only duplicate, no claim-matched mechanism, or source not recoverable enough to assess. Low-quality or unreviewed records may be retained as defeating/adjacent evidence but cannot alone establish the positive novelty claim.

Screening occurs title -> abstract/official description -> accessible full text for strongest predecessors. Every exclusion from the shortlist must retain a reason. Deduplication uses the case adapter's normalized-title/source/date rule; DOI and arXiv identifiers are reconciled manually for the final shortlist.

## Citation chaining

Backward and forward chaining is required for LATTICE, NabaOS, the Action Evidence Package freshness work, opML, CommitLLM, EigenAI, and the closest smart-contract provenance predecessor. Direct citation edges must be verified from a reference list or citation-index record; thematic succession is not a citation edge.

Known-item recovery must succeed for opML and at least four of the new claim-matched assurance/receipt works before the novelty gate can close.

## Access limits

- No proprietary Scopus or Web of Science access is assumed.
- Google Patents public pages do not establish complete family or claim coverage.
- IEEE and other standards full text may be paywalled.
- Search-engine indexing can omit private, unpublished, non-English, or differently termed work.
- Crossref/OpenAlex metadata may be incomplete or noisy and is not full-text evidence.
- The independent human search challenge and accountable patent/standards review are not yet complete.
