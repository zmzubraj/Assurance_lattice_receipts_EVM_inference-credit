# Article type

## Frontiers Technology and Code

The official description permits new technology/code, new applications of known technology/software, and implementations in novel settings. It requires novelty, human-readable documentation, an online repository with DOI/URI, project link, operating system, programming language, restrictions/license information, examples/results, scalability, and limitations. Maximum word count is 12,000. The current A-type APC is CHF 2,195, payable on acceptance; fee support may exist but is not assumed.

## IEEE Access Research Article

The official submission checklist requires the IEEE Access double-column single-spaced template, matching source and PDF files under 40 MB, AI-generated-content disclosure and identification, a complete and stable author list, public populated ORCID for the submitting author, biographies for every author, checked references, and no simultaneous submission. The official site states continuous publication, binary peer review, and an APC of USD 2,160 plus applicable taxes.
