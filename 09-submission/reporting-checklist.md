# Guideline

No universal domain reporting guideline equivalent to CONSORT or PRISMA was identified for this software-only finite-state blockchain systems study. The governing checklist is therefore the selected venue’s current author instructions plus the project-specific engineering/systems transparency checklist below. This routing decision requires human confirmation.
